# Content plan — TFSA/RA explainers

Drafted 2026-08-31, per the Piggybank competitive-analysis report's #5 recommendation
("start light content now, even pre-scale"). Piggybank scored 1/5 on thought leadership in
that benchmark — tied with its newest, least-credible Direct competitor (Money Tracker SA).
EasyEquities' TFSA blog content was the evidence that this dimension is both cheap to move and
genuinely persuasive for this exact audience.

**Scope of this doc:** three outlines only, not finished posts — each angled at the specific
white-space finding from the report (nobody else combines breadth + TFSA/RA depth), not generic
"what is a TFSA" filler that any of the six researched competitors could already publish.

---

## 1. "Why your TFSA app and your budget app are the same problem"

**Angle:** Direct restatement of the report's headline finding, written for a reader, not an
investor. Most people track spending in one app and their TFSA in another (or nowhere) — walks
through why that split makes the R46,000 annual limit and R500,000 lifetime limit easy to
blow past by accident, because no single view shows contributions *and* the rest of your
cashflow together.

**Proof point to include:** the actual 2026 limits (R46,000/year, R500,000 lifetime, 40% SARS
penalty on excess) — same figures verified against EasyEquities' own support docs this
session, so this piece can cite a real number, not a vague warning.

**Call to action:** "See your TFSA contributions next to everything else you own" → app.

---

## 2. "What actually counts toward your TFSA limit (and what doesn't)"

**Angle:** A common, genuine point of confusion — growth/interest/dividends don't count toward
the limit, only what you deposit does. This is a real explainer with a real wrong answer people
give, not manufactured controversy.

**Proof point to include:** cite SARS's own rule structure (deposits only, not growth) —
requires one verification pass against SARS or a reputable SA source before publishing; not
independently confirmed in this session, flagged rather than guessed.

**Call to action:** soft — this one earns trust more than it drives installs.

---

## 3. "Retirement Annuity vs. TFSA: they're not competing, they're different jobs"

**Angle:** RA (tax-deductible now, taxed on withdrawal, locked until 55) vs. TFSA (no upfront
deduction, tax-free forever, accessible anytime) — a genuinely useful comparison for someone
deciding where to put their next R1,000, not a listicle.

**Proof point to include:** Piggybank tracks both side by side in one ledger view — this is the
one place the content can legitimately point back at the product without it reading as an ad,
since the comparison itself is the reason a combined view is useful.

**Call to action:** "Track both in one place" → app.

---

## Before publishing any of these

- Piece 2's SARS-rule claim needs independent verification — do not publish it on the strength
  of this session's research alone.
- None of these should reference competitors by name (unlike FinWise's approach) until
  Piggybank has its own evidence base to stand on — per the competitive report, that's still a
  gap, not a strength, this early.
- Where to publish (own blog, Medium, LinkedIn, or held until launch) is an open decision, not
  made in this session.
